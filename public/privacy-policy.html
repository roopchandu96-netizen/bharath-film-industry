1. Information We Collect

To provide film finance, script submissions, community networking, ticket booking, and decentralized filmmaking services, we collect the following categories of information:

A. Personal Information You Explicitly Provide

  • Account Registration Information: When creating an account, we collect your full name, email address, mobile phone number (WhatsApp enabled), and encrypted credentials.
  • Verification & KYC Data: To comply with anti-fraud guidelines and authenticate institutional producer credits or investor agreements, we may collect government ID proofs, address verification, and digital signatures.
  • Financial & Transaction Information: When you participate in investments, buy movie tickets, or fund film projects, we collect transaction identifiers, payment amounts, and bank transfer reference slips. We do not store raw debit/credit card numbers or net-banking passwords; payment processing is handled securely by RBI-authorized payment aggregators (e.g., Razorpay).
  • Creative Submissions: Film scripts, loglines, synopses, production budgets, and pitch decks submitted by filmmakers and directors.

B. Automatically Collected Device & Technical Data

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and network connection status to ensure app stability and prevent unauthorized access.
  • Log & Diagnostics Data: Crash logs, system error reports, performance timestamps, and interaction logs within the app to diagnose technical bugs and enhance security.

C. App Permissions

  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET): Required to synchronize real-time project updates, communicate with backend database infrastructure, and process authenticated transactions.
  • Storage / Media Access: Optional, requested only when you upload a script PDF, profile picture, or payment confirmation receipt.

2. Purpose and Use of Your Data

We use the data we collect strictly for legitimate, transparent purposes:

  1. Service Delivery: To manage user accounts, display film projects, enable script submissions, and allocate producer tiers and movie tickets.
  2. Transaction Verification: To reconcile investment records, process payments, and record verified ledger entries.
  3. Identity Verification & Regulatory Compliance: To verify user identities (KYC), prevent fraudulent activities, and maintain platform security.
  4. User Communication: To send transaction confirmations, account notifications, WhatsApp customer support updates, and platform announcements.
  5. Platform Protection: To monitor for abusive behavior, enforce our Terms of Service, and safeguard intellectual property rights.

3. Data Storage, Retention, and Security

  • Encryption in Transit & at Rest: All communications between the Bharat Film Industry mobile application and our servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS). Sensitive database assets are encrypted at rest using industry-standard cloud database infrastructure (Supabase / Firebase).
  • Data Retention Period: We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active or as required to deliver our services. If you delete your account, personal data is purged subject only to legal and statutory retention requirements.
  • Statutory Retention: Under applicable Indian commercial and tax laws, financial transaction logs and tax-related receipts must be retained securely for the statutory record-keeping period.

4. Third-Party Sharing and Disclosures

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to advertisers or commercial third parties. Data is shared only with verified service providers necessary to operate the application:

  • Cloud Infrastructure & Database: Supabase and Firebase for secure database hosting, user authentication, and encrypted data storage.
  • Payment Gateways: Razorpay for processing digital payment transactions under PCI-DSS certified standards.
  • Legal & Regulatory Authorities: Only when strictly required by a valid court order, government subpoena, or legal statute to protect against fraud or harm.

5. User Rights & Account / Data Deletion

Every user of Bharat Film Industry has full control over their personal information, including the right to access, rectify, and delete their data.

How to Delete Your Account and Personal Data:

You can request complete deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time through either of the following methods:

  • Inside the App: Open the App → Go to Profile → Scroll down to the Danger Zone → Tap "Revoke Institutional Node" (Delete Account).
  • Via Dedicated Web Request: Visit our dedicated deletion page: https://www.bfiiy.com/delete-account.html.
  • Via Email: Send an email from your registered address to bharatfilmindustry@gmail.com with the subject line "Account and Data Deletion Request".

Deletion requests are processed within 7 to 30 days. You will receive an official confirmation once your account and all associated personal information have been erased.

We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal data from individuals under 18 years of age. If we learn that a minor has provided us with personal information, we take immediate steps to delete such data from our systems.
