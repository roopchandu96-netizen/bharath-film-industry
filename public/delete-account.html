Account & Data Deletion Request

App: Bharat Film Industry (com.bharatfilmindustry.app1)

In accordance with Google Play's User Data Policy, users of the Bharat Film Industry application have the right to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

Option 1: Delete Directly Inside the App (Instant)

  1. Open the Bharat Film Industry App on your device.
  2. Log in to your account.
  3. Navigate to the Profile tab.
  4. Scroll down to the Danger Zone section.
  5. Tap "Revoke Institutional Node" (Delete Account) and confirm your request.

Option 2: Submit a Deletion Request via Email (Web)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot log in, you can request account and data deletion by emailing us directly:

  • Recipient Email: bharatfilmindustry@gmail.com
  • Subject Line: Account and Data Deletion Request - Bharat Film Industry
  • Required Information:
    • Your Registered Full Name

Send Deletion Request Email

What Data Is Deleted?

  • Account Credentials: User ID, authentication profile, and hashed password.
  • Personal Information: Full name, email address, phone number, and KYC documents.
  • Device & Log Data: Device identifiers and system activity logs.

What Data May Be Retained?

In compliance with Indian financial regulations, tax auditing laws, and anti-money laundering (AML) statutes, records of completed financial transactions, invoices, and signed investment contracts cannot be immediately expunged and will be retained securely solely for the statutory period required by law.

Processing Timeline

Deletion requests submitted via email are verified and processed within 7 to 30 business days. You will receive an email confirmation once your account and personal data have been permanently purged.
