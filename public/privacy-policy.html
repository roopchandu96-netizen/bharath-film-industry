Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how we collect, use, and safeguard your data.

Application: Bharat Film Industry
Package ID: com.bharatfilmindustry.app1
Operating Entity: Bharat Film Industry
Effective Date: June 23, 2026 (Updated: Oct 2026)
1

Information We Collect

To provide film finance, script submissions, community networking, ticket booking, and decentralized filmmaking services, we collect the following categories of information:

A. Personal Information You Explicitly Provide

  • Account Registration Details: When you register, we collect your full name, email address, mobile phone number (WhatsApp enabled), and encrypted credentials.
  • Verification & KYC Data: To comply with financial regulations and establish accredited producer credits or investment agreements, we collect government ID proofs, address verification, and digital signatures.
  • Financial & Investment Data: Investment amounts, transaction receipts, bank transfer confirmation slips, and payment details you provide to facilitate and verify investments. Raw debit/credit card credentials are never stored by us; payments are securely handled by RBI-authorized payment aggregators (e.g., Razorpay).
  • Creative Submissions: Screenplays, stories, loglines, synopses, and production budgets uploaded by filmmakers and directors.

B. Automatically Collected Technical Data

  • Device Identifiers: Mobile device ID, operating system version, device model, and IP address to optimize performance and prevent unauthorized account access.
  • Log & Diagnostics Data: System crash events, page visits, and actions taken within the App to monitor platform stability and security.

C. Permissions

  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET): Required to sync with our database services (Supabase/Firebase) and process investments in real time.
  • Storage / Media: Requested only when you upload a script PDF, profile photo, or payment proof slip.
2

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for specific, transparent, and legitimate purposes:

  1. To Provide and Maintain Our Service: Managing user registration, showing active projects, booking movie tickets, and tracking producer tiers.
  2. To Facilitate Investments: Verifying payment confirmations, recording verified ledger entries, and awarding appropriate Producer Tiers.
  3. To Verify Identity (KYC): Ensuring transactions are legitimate and preventing financial fraud on the platform.
  4. To Communicate with You: Sending notifications, WhatsApp support messages, and transaction updates.
  5. To Ensure App Security: Monitoring for suspicious activity, protecting user accounts, and enforcing our terms.
3

Data Storage, Retention, and Security

  • Encrypted in Transit & at Rest: All database transactions and user credentials are encrypted via HTTPS/TLS during transmission and stored securely using industry-standard database providers (Supabase / Firebase).
  • Data Retention: We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to deliver our services.
  • Statutory Retention: In compliance with Indian commercial and tax statutes, records of completed financial transactions are retained securely for the legally mandated period.
4

Third-Party Sharing and Disclosures

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties. We only share information with:

  • Cloud & Database Providers: Supabase and Firebase for authentication, database hosting, and secure encrypted storage.
  • Payment Processors: Razorpay for processing digital payment transactions under certified PCI-DSS standards.
  • Legal & Regulatory Authorities: When required by applicable Indian law or court order to prevent financial fraud.
5

Account & Personal Data Deletion

Every user of Bharat Film Industry has full control over their personal information and may request complete account and data deletion at any time:

Deletion Options:

  • Directly In-App: Open the App → Go to Profile → Scroll down to Danger Zone → Tap "Revoke Institutional Node" (Delete Account).
  • Dedicated Web Page: Submit an instant request at https://www.bfiiy.com/data-deletion.
  • Via Support Email: Send an email from your registered address to bharatfilmindustry@gmail.com.

Deletion requests are processed and confirmed within 7 to 30 days.

6

Children's Privacy

Our services are strictly intended for individuals aged 18 and older due to the commercial and contractual nature of film financing. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If we discover that a minor has provided us with personal data, we delete it immediately.

7

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the Effective Date at the top.
