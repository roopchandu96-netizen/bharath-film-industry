User Data Rights & Compliance

Account & Data Deletion

In compliance with Google Play's User Data Policy, users of the Bharat Film Industry application have the right to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data.

Application: Bharat Film Industry
Package ID: com.bharatfilmindustry.app1
Processing Timeline: 7 to 30 Business Days
Support: bharatfilmindustry@gmail.com
1

Delete Directly Inside the App (Instant)

If you have the Bharat Film Industry app installed on your device:

  1. Open the Bharat Film Industry App.
  2. Log in to your account.
  3. Tap the Profile tab in the navigation bar.
  4. Scroll down to the Danger Zone section.
  5. Tap "Revoke Institutional Node" (Delete Account) and confirm.
2

Request Deletion via Web / Email

If you uninstalled the application or cannot access your account, submit a deletion request via email:

  • Recipient: bharatfilmindustry@gmail.com
  • Subject Line: Account and Data Deletion Request - Bharat Film Industry
  • Required Details: Your registered Name, Email Address, and Phone Number.
Send Deletion Request Email
3

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

Data That Is Permanently Deleted:

  • Account profile, full name, email address, and phone number.
  • Stored authentication tokens and password hashes.
  • Device identifiers and activity log entries.

Data That May Be Retained:

Under Indian financial, tax auditing, and regulatory statutes, records of completed monetary investments and signed legal agreements must be retained securely for statutory record-keeping periods.
