BFI Governance & Risk Disclosures

Terms & Conditions

Please review the official rules, regulations, and risk disclosures governing the Bharat Film Industry ecosystem.

1

Platform Role

Bharat Film Industry (BFI) acts as a facilitator connecting investors, filmmakers, directors, producers, and film projects. BFI does not guarantee the commercial success of any film project.

2

Communication Policy (For Investors)

All communications regarding film projects, investments, agreements, budgets, and returns must be conducted exclusively through the BFI team.

STRICTLY PROHIBITED: Investors are strictly prohibited from directly negotiating, funding, or entering into separate agreements with directors, writers, producers, or project representatives outside the BFI platform. Any violation may result in termination of investor participation and cancellation of associated benefits.
3

Investment Risk Disclosure

Film investment is a high-risk business activity. Investors acknowledge and agree that:

  • Returns are entirely dependent on the commercial performance of the film.
  • Revenue depends on theatrical collections, OTT rights, satellite rights, digital rights, overseas distribution, and other exploitation channels.
  • BFI does not guarantee profits, fixed returns, minimum returns, or capital protection.
4

Return on Investment

Investor returns will be distributed according to the agreed revenue-sharing structure of the specific project. The amount payable to investors shall be determined only after actual revenue realization. BFI makes no promise or representation regarding specific profit percentages, fixed returns, or guaranteed timelines for returns.

5

Loss Acknowledgment

Investors understand that film projects may generate lower-than-expected revenue or incur losses. BFI shall not be held responsible for any financial losses arising from a project's commercial performance.

6

Script Registration (For Directors)

Before submission to BFI, every screenplay, story, script, dialogue draft, or creative work must be officially registered with the appropriate copyright or writers' association authority. BFI shall not be responsible for ownership disputes involving unregistered content.

7

Budget Accuracy & Production Responsibility

Directors and producers must provide realistic and accurate project budgets. If actual production cost exceeds approved budget estimates, BFI is not obligated to arrange additional funding, and bears no responsibility for overruns. The production team remains solely responsible for execution, crew management, legal compliance, and delivery.

8

Acceptance & Inquiries
